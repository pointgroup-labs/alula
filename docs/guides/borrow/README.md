# Borrow

Borrowing lets you take an asset out of a pool (for example, USDC) by using your other assets as collateral. While your borrow is open, you pay interest, so the amount you owe can increase over time until you repay. How much you can borrow depends on your collateral and the pool’s risk settings, especially Open LTV (your borrowing limit) and Close LTV (the level where your position can become eligible for liquidation).
