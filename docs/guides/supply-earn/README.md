# Supply (Earn)

Supplying means depositing an asset into an Alula pool so other users can borrow it. In return, you earn Supply APY, which can change over time based on market activity, especially utilization (how much of the pool is currently borrowed). Higher utilization typically means borrowers are paying more, which can increase yield for suppliers.

Your supply is recorded on-chain in the protocol’s smart contracts and associated with your wallet address. You can withdraw it at any time, subject to pool conditions. If the pool is heavily utilized or temporary limits are active, you might not be able to withdraw your full balance immediately.
