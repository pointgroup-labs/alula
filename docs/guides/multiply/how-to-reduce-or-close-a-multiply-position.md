# How to Reduce or Close a Multiply Position

Withdrawing from a Multiply position reduces the size of your position. Depending on the vault’s configuration, the protocol may reduce your collateral and debt proportionally to maintain your leverage ratio. A full withdrawal closes the position.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
Go to **Multiply** or **My Account**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Press **Withdraw** for the vault, where you want to reduce or close your Multiply position.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
In the **Withdraw** window, enter an amount (or press **MAX**).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Review the details:

* **Operation Fee:** A protocol fee charged for the action.
* **Transaction Fee:** The network fee to submit the transaction on Stellar.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Press **Withdraw** and confirm the tranaction in your wallet.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
