# Multiply

Multiply lets you increase your exposure to an asset in one flow. Instead of borrowing and supplying repeatedly, Alula bundles the steps into a single action: it borrows an asset, adds it to your position, and sets your final collateral and debt at once. This can create a larger position than a simple supply, but it also increases risk, which means your gains and losses can move faster, and your position can become eligible for liquidation sooner if prices move against you or interest adds to your debt.
