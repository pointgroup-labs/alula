# Architecture Components

<table><thead><tr><th width="98.3636474609375">Component</th><th width="144.727294921875">What it is</th><th width="298.99993896484375">What it does</th><th>How it interacts</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Market Manager admin</td><td>Protocol-level administrative role controlled via a multisig</td><td><ul><li>Deploys new market contracts through the manager</li><li>Reads the list of deployed markets</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Calls the Market Manager contract</li><li>Operates only at the “which markets exist” layer</li><li>Does not touch pools or user obligations directly</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Market Admin</td><td>Administrative actor for a specific (owned) market</td><td><ul><li>Configures the market with <code>initialize_pool</code>, <code>initialize_multiply_pair</code></li><li>Manages pool config with <code>queue_in_pool_config_update</code>, <code>apply_pool_config_update</code>, <code>cancel_pool_config_update</code></li><li>Controls market status with <code>update_market_status</code></li><li>Triggers distribution or withdraws fees where authorized; protocol beneficiary allocations are settled via the permissionless <code>distribute</code> method</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Calls the target Market contract directly</li><li>Changes are persisted in the market’s pool structure</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>User</td><td>Standard protocol participant (lender/borrower/leveraged trader/reader)</td><td><ul><li>Supplies and earns with <code>deposit</code>, <code>deposit_into_earn_obligation</code></li><li>Borrows with <code>borrow</code> and repays with <code>repay</code></li><li>Exits with <code>withdraw</code>, <code>withdraw_from_earn_obligation</code></li><li>Manages collateral with <code>add_collateral</code>, <code>remove_collateral</code></li><li>Uses leverage with <code>deposit_with_leverage</code>, <code>withdraw_from_leveraged</code></li><li>Reads state with view methods (e.g. <code>get_user_obligation</code>, <code>get_pool</code>)</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Sends transactions to the Market contract</li><li>Market updates the user’s obligation and relevant asset pool</li><li>Market applies current pool config/state to the action</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Market Manager</td><td>Registry and factory for markets</td><td><ul><li>Deploys new markets (constructor invoked via manager)</li><li>Stores and returns the list of deployed markets</li><li>Serves as protocol-level entry point for market creation</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Receives calls from Market Manager admin</li><li>Produces new Market instances</li><li>Exposes deployed market addresses to other actors</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Markets</td><td>Main functional contract of the protocol. </td><td><p>There are 2 types of markets: </p><ul><li>Owned markets: Markets whose config can be modified by its admin via putting a config update to the update queue. Admins of this type of market can only redeem market fees.</li></ul><p>Functions</p><ul><li>Maintains per-asset pools created with <code>initialize_pool</code></li><li>Maintains pool config and pool state</li><li>Stores and updates all user obligations (plain/earn/multiply) on calls like <code>deposit, borrow, repay, withdraw, add_collateral, remove_collateral</code></li><li>Executes advanced flows: <code>deposit_with_leverage, withdraw_from_leveraged, flash_loan</code></li><li>Provides service functions: <code>liquidate, cover_obligation_bad_debt</code></li><li>Enforces market status and limits</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Receives calls from User and Market Admin</li><li>Is created by the Market Manager</li><li>Calls external DEX for swaps in leveraged flows</li><li>Queries external oracle for prices</li><li>Accepts liquidation calls from external liquidators</li><li>Applies cross-pool decisions to obligations</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Asset pool</td><td>Per-asset loan pools inside a market (e.g. XLM, USD)</td><td><ul><li>Hold supply and borrow balances for their asset </li><li>Act as targets for user deposits and borrows</li><li>Can participate in collateral relationships between assets</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Their balances and utilization are tracked in pool state</li><li>User obligations record into specific asset pools</li><li>Pool structure before any loss is socialized to lenders</td><td><ul><li>Receives top-ups from utilization-triggered withdrawal throttles</li><li>When an insolvent obligation remains under-collateralized after liquidations, the market requests coverage from the fund, and only if it’s insufficient is any remaining shortfall socialized across the pool’s lenders</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Pool structure (pool config → pool state)</td><td>Internal representation of pool rules and live figures</td><td><ul><li>Pool config stores fee/health/interest/accrual params; updated via <code>queue_in_pool_config_update</code> and <code>apply_pool_config_update</code></li><li>Pool state stores live numbers: available, total borrowed, total jTokens/dTokens, collateral, accumulated fees</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Receives updates from market-admin actions</li><li>Receives operational updates from user actions processed by the market</li><li>Tracks asset pools as the single source of truth for totals and fees</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>User/<em>Multiply</em> obligations</td><td>Unified storage for all user positions in a market</td><td><ul><li>Stores deposits, collateral, and <code>MinCollateralValue</code></li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Updated whenever a user calls market methods (<code>deposit</code>, <code>borrow</code>, <code>repay</code>, <code>withdraw</code>, <code>add_collateral</code>, leveraged methods)</li><li>Records into the corresponding asset pools</li><li>Evaluated by cross-pool logic for health and liquidation</li><li>Target of liquidations initiated through the market</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Cross-pool</td><td>Shared risk and health logic across pools</td><td><ul><li>Performs cross-pool collateral enablement (CPC)</li><li>Computes position health (HF) using pool/oracle data</li><li>Determines liquidation conditions (LIQ) for unhealthy obligations</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Operates on user/<em>Multiply</em> obligations</li><li>Decisions are enforced when a liquidator calls liquidate on the market</li><li>Relies on pool state and oracle prices made available by the market</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>AMM DEX</td><td>External swap venue (e.g. Soroswap Router/Pair)</td><td><ul><li>Executes swaps that are part of leveraged deposit/withdraw sequences</li><li>Supports flash-loan-based operations that need token conversion</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Called by the market during methods like <code>deposit_with_leverage</code>, <code>withdraw_from_leveraged</code></li><li>Does not initiate interactions on its own</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Oracle</td><td>External SEP-40 price oracle</td><td><ul><li>Returns current asset prices for pools</li><li>Enables valuation of collateral and debt</li><li>Supports health checks and liquidation decisions</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Queried by the market whenever up-to-date prices are needed (borrow, withdraw, liquidate)</li><li>Provides price inputs used by cross-pool logic</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Liquidator</td><td>External, permissionless actor (not the borrower)</td><td><ul><li>Calls the market’s <code>liquidate</code> method on unhealthy positions</li><li>Repays debt and receives discounted collateral (e.g. 5% bonus)</li><li>Participates in a competitive liquidation market</li></ul></td><td><ul><li>Sends liquidation transactions to the market</li><li>Market applies cross-pool liquidation rules</li><li>Market updates user/<em>Multiply</em> obligations and affected asset pools</li></ul></td></tr></tbody></table>
