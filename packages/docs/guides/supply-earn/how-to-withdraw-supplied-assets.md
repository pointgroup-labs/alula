# How to Withdraw Supplied Assets

1. Go to **My Account**.
2. Under **Your Supplies**, press **Withdraw** for the asset you want.
3. In the **Withdraw** window:

   * Enter an amount (or press **MAX**).
   * Review the details:
     * **Available for Withdrawal**: What you can withdraw right now.
     * **Pool Withdrawal Limit**: A pool-level cap that can limit withdrawals.
     * **Health Factor**: A safety indicator that can drop if you withdraw collateral that supports borrowing.

4. Press **Withdraw** and confirm in your wallet.
