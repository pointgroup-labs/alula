# How to Multiply Assets

1. Go to **Multiply**.
2. Press **Multiply** for the vault you want.
3. Enter an amount (or press **MAX**).
4. Choose your leverage using the **Multiplier** slider. As you move the slider, Alula updates the estimates for the position.
5. Review the details:

   * **Liquidity Available:** How much the pool can currently lend for the loop. If liquidity is low, you may not be able to open the position at your chosen multiplier.
   * **Max Multiplied Amount:** The maximum position size you can reach based on your deposit, the selected multiplier, and available liquidity.
   * **Operation Fee**: A protocol fee charged on certain actions. For borrows, this fee may be added to your loan principal. These fees are routed to protocol beneficiaries, such as the insurance fund or treasury. In some cases, a portion may be paid to a referrer.
   * **Transaction Fee**: The network fee required to submit the transaction.

6. Press **Multiply** to open the position and confirm the transaction in your wallet.
