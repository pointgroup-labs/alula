# How to Repay Borrows

1. Go to **My Account**.
2. Under **Your Borrows**, press **Repay** for the asset you want.
3. Enter an amount (or select **MAX**).
4. Review **Borrow Balance after Repayment**. This shows what your remaining debt would be if you proceed with the repayment amount you entered.
5. Press **Repay** and confirm the transaction in your wallet.
